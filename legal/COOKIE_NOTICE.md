# Cookie Notice

**Africa University Student Portfolio & Recommendation System**
**Last updated:** 3 November 2026

This notice explains how the System uses cookies and similar browser storage technologies. It should be read with our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. Similar technologies, such as `localStorage` and `sessionStorage`, also store small pieces of data in your browser.

## 2. What we use

We use **only strictly necessary** storage. These items are required for the System to work and cannot be switched off in the System.

| Name / type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Authentication token (JWT) | Keeps you signed in and proves your identity and role to the server | Until it expires or you sign out |
| Session / interface state | Remembers your current view and form progress in the single-page app | Until you close the tab or sign out |
| Security cookies (if used) | Protects against forgery and misuse | Session |

## 3. What we do not use

- No advertising or marketing cookies.
- No cross-site tracking.
- No sale or sharing of browsing data with advertisers.

If we add analytics in future, we will update this notice and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

## 4. Managing cookies

Because we only use strictly necessary storage, we do not show a consent choice for it. You can still clear or block cookies and site data in your browser settings, but if you do, you will be signed out and the System may not work properly.

Help pages for common browsers:
- Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
- Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
- Safari: Settings > Privacy > Manage Website Data
- Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions

## 5. Third parties

The frontend (Vercel) and backend (Render) hosts may set technical cookies or log requests to deliver and secure the service. We do not allow them to use this data for advertising.

We may update this notice. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
