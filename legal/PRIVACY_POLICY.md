# Privacy Policy

**Africa University Student Portfolio & Recommendation System**
**Last updated:** 3 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Africa University ("the University", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information when you use the Student Portfolio & Recommendation System ("the System"). It applies to students, staff, supervisors and administrators.

We process personal data in line with the Zimbabwe Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] and other applicable laws.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Africa University, Mutare, Zimbabwe.

Contact for privacy matters: **[privacy@africau.edu – replace with the official address]**

## 2. Information we collect

**Account and identity information**
- Full name, student or staff number, university email address, role (`student`, `staff`, `supervisor`, `admin`), faculty/department and programme.
- Password, stored only as a salted bcrypt hash. We never store or see your plain-text password.

**Registration verification information**
- Details you submit during registration, which we check against official University registry records.
- One-time verification codes sent to your email, and the administrator's approval or rejection decision.

**Portfolio and academic information**
- Items you add to your portfolio: projects, achievements, skills, experience, documents and descriptions.
- Academic information needed to support recommendations, as provided or confirmed by the University.

**Recommendation letter information**
- Letter requests, drafts, final letters, approval status and related messages between students and lecturers/supervisors.

**Technical information**
- Authentication tokens, IP address, browser/device type, request logs and error logs, used for security and troubleshooting.

## 3. How we use your information

- To create and manage your account and verify that you are a genuine member of the University.
- To operate your portfolio and the recommendation letter workflow.
- To send service emails: verification codes, request notices, approvals and status changes.
- To generate AI-assisted drafts and portfolio audits (see section 5).
- To protect the System from fraud, abuse and unauthorised access.
- To meet legal, academic-record and regulatory obligations.

Our lawful bases are: performance of our relationship with you as a student or staff member, our legitimate interest in running and securing the System, compliance with legal obligations, and your consent where we ask for it.

## 4. Who can see your information

- **You** can see your own account and portfolio.
- **Lecturers, supervisors and staff** can see the portfolio information and requests relevant to letters they are asked to write.
- **Administrators** can see account and registration data needed to approve users and manage the System.
- **Recipients you choose**, such as employers or institutions, receive a recommendation letter only when the letter is issued.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 5. Artificial intelligence (Google Gemini)

The System uses Google's Gemini AI to help lecturers draft recommendation letters and to produce portfolio audits.

- Relevant portfolio and request content is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate the output.
- AI output is a **draft**. A lecturer or supervisor reviews, edits and takes responsibility for any letter before it is issued.
- We do not make solely automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.
- Please avoid putting sensitive information (health details, national ID numbers, financial data) into free-text portfolio fields, as this text may be passed to the AI service.

## 7. How long we keep data

- **Account and portfolio data:** while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards in line with University records policy.
- **Recommendation letters and request history:** kept as part of the academic record for as long as the University's retention policy requires.
- **Verification codes:** short-lived and deleted or expired shortly after use.
- **Server and security logs:** kept for a limited period, typically no more than 90 days.

## 8. Security

We use measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, signed JWT tokens with expiry, role-based access control and restricted database access. No system is completely secure. Keep your password confidential and tell us immediately if you suspect unauthorised access.

## 9. Your rights

Subject to the law, you may:
- Ask for access to the personal data we hold about you.
- Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Ask us to delete data, where we are not required to keep it.
- Object to or ask us to restrict certain processing.
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
- Complain to the data protection authority (POTRAZ, the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe) if you believe your rights have been infringed.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address in section 1. We may need to verify your identity first.

## 10. Children

The System is intended for University students, staff and supervisors, who are adults or admitted students. It is not directed at children.
